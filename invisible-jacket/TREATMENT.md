# The Invisible Jacket: treatment (as built)

Style: Crayon Picture Book (`styles/crayon-book/STYLE.md`). 45 s, 1920×1080, 24 fps, English narration with burned-in subtitles.

## Logline
Walking home from school, a boy finds an invisible jacket hidden in a hedge. He slips it on to look into the neighbour's garden, and learns that the wonderful thing to see is the one he is allowed to share: he takes the jacket off and leaves a kind note.

## Structure (one continuous page, one camera)
The page is one long picture drawn live. The camera moves along it, and page-like beats are marked by the camera moving to a new part of the picture (a sketchbook of five pages was considered and dropped: one continuous page fits the crayon engine better).

| Time | Beat | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | Hook | Paper, the title "The Invisible Jacket" written on the sky |
| 3–10 s | Walk | The boy walks right along the path, from his small house toward the hedge |
| 10–14.5 s | Discovery | He stands in the gap. The white-wax jacket is invisible until the watercolour wash passes over it at 12 s, revealing it |
| 14.5–17.5 s | Invisible | He puts the jacket on. He becomes a faint dotted outline and walks through the gap |
| 17.5–34.5 s | Garden | The outline wanders to the fence. The garden colours in: a pool (turned blue by a wash at 21 s), a rabbit, a sheep, a turtle and a bird |
| 34.5–36.5 s | Realisation | The outline walks back to the gate. He stops and thinks |
| 36.2–38 s | Kindness | He solid again, takes the jacket off, hangs it back on the branch, and writes a note |
| 38–40.5 s | Note | "Welcome home" is written in crayon and tucked on the grass by the gate |
| 40.6–45 s | Ending | The neighbour waves from beside the pool; the boy waves back; the camera pulls out |

## Native moves used
- **Wax resist:** the jacket is drawn in white crayon, hidden until the wash passes (the reveal).
- **Drawn live:** the fence, the hedge and the garden colour in as the camera passes.
- **Colouring is an action:** the pool is coloured in, and the wash turns it blue.
- **Boil:** calmed for the wordless garden passage.

## Narration (lines, with start times)
1. 0.9 s: Walking home from school, a boy kicked a stone.
2. 10.9 s: Behind the hedge, he found a jacket.
3. 15.2 s: It made him invisible.
4. 18.2 s: So he peeked into the neighbour's garden.
5. 24.2 s: A pool, a rabbit, a sheep and a turtle.
6. 31.8 s: Then he realised: this is someone's home.
7. 36.2 s: He hung the jacket back and left a note.
8. 40.6 s: Next morning, the neighbour waved.

## Sound
- Score: an original 72 BPM, F-major tune in music box, with a kalimba arpeggio under it all. The theme returns, quieter, in the wordless part and resolves at the end.
- Foley: footsteps on the path, crayon scratches, a wet brush sweep for each wash, a ding when the jacket shows, a pop and swish for putting it on and taking it off, a splash, small animal sounds and birds.
- Mix: the score ducks under the narration; loudness −14 LUFS.

## Content note
The jacket lets the boy watch someone without being seen. The film makes that a mistake he corrects: he takes the jacket off and leaves a kind note, so the story ends with openness, not spying.
